# Problem statement — template (fill AFTER the team picks a problem)

**Do not fill this during early framestorm.** Decide what problem you are on first. Then write one
statement in the shape below.

## What this statement must enable

| Goal | Question the PS must let us ask |
|---|---|
| **90-day move** | Can Synvitta **move it in 90 days?** |
| **How big?** | What is the **value of being right** on this first move? |

How big ≠ nationwide market sizing (case out). Value of being right = payoff of a correct first
foothold / proof / path — without naming a product or cause in the question.

## Required shape

> What should **[decision maker]** do to **[measurable result]** by **[time]**, given
> **[constraint]**, without **[boundary]**?

| Slot | This case | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Decision maker | Synvitta’s leadership | Fixed unless team names a narrower owner |
| Measurable result | _observable outcome — not a product name_ | Must be something you can move in ~90 days **and** that carries value-of-being-right |
| Time | _by when_ | Default: **90 days** (case window) |
| Constraint | _e.g. limited resources, no FDA clinical authorization_ | |
| Boundary | _e.g. without FDA filing strategy / without assuming clinical marketing rights_ | |

## Five tests (all must pass)

| # | Test | Pass? | Exact words that fail (if any) |

| 5 | No built-in answer — **no product or cause named** | | |

**Hard rule:** do not put assumptions of the answer in the question. Products, channels, customer
types as *the* solution, and root causes belong in hypotheses — not in the statement.
