## How to sell “premium next-gen” without unsupported H2H

**Thesis (ESTIMATED):** premium = next-gen platform fit for hormone testing (precision + speed *as eval questions*), not “we already beat Thermo on cost/accuracy.”

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Lead with **eval proof ask**: “In a research eval, which analytical specificity and/or assay TAT proxies would make you open a paid custom SOW vs staying with your incumbent?” | Claim peer-beating **cost** or **clinical accuracy** without Synvitta-supplied H2H (**Unsupported**) |
| State Synvitta marketing only as **proxy with limits**; ask Synvitta for any internal comparable metrics before outreach | Invent head-to-head numbers |
| Keep language: research / assay-development / co-dev · RUO boundaries | Clinical DTC, patient diagnosis, “validate then offer clinically” (**RETRIEVED** F-RUO-5) |
| Position premium as **willingness to pay for a scoped proof**, then paid SOW | Free forever pilot as the offer |

**Proof ask (put on the eval sheet):** named marker set · 1–2 analytical proxies (e.g. specificity / cross-reactivity class; assay TAT or hands-on time) · pass/fail thresholds the counterpart writes · claim regime = research/development only.

**Q: Why would a Thermo or Agilent talk to a small Brazilian platform instead of building in-house?**  
**A:** We are not selling “replace your catalog.” We are selling a **scoped research eval → paid custom SOW** for a hormone-shaped gap they will not prioritize this quarter. If they will not name pass/fail criteria under research claims, Route B dies — that is a feature of the plan, not a bug.

**Q: Isn’t “premium next-gen” just marketing?**  
**A:** Yes until the eval. Premium is the **price of a proof**, not a claimed H2H win. The eval sheet forces *their* specificity/TAT proxies; we will not invent peer-beating accuracy or cost numbers.

**Q: What if the eval never converts?**  
**A:** Then we kill the beachhead motion at day 90. Free pilot forever is an explicit kill — same as clinical-claim drift.

**Q: Who is Pancher?**  
**A:** Team label — **not locked**. Best candidates are PerkinElmer/Revvity or a misheard platform brand; we confirm before Prompt 5 outreach rather than fake precision.

**Q: Are you recommending an FDA path?**  
**A:** No. We verify one assumption: research/dev + paid SOW under allowed claims. Filing strategy is out of scope.
