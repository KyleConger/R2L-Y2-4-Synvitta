## A5 — Metrics, kills & skeptical Q&A (incl. Thermo as ProcartaPlex peer)

**Takeaway title:** Day-90 success is paid motion or a written kill — free pilots and clinical drift fail the plan.

### Metrics (2)

| # | Metric | 90-day target | Early warning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M1** | Qualified conversations (named role + hormone/assay-dev context + claim regime) | ≥3 by day 45 · **both** Thermo and Abbott attempted by day 60 | Week 4: <2 talks booked |
| **M2** | Conversion signal | ≥1 eval accepted **in principle** by day 60 · **paid** SOW / discovery by day 90 (binary) | Day 75: eval open with no SOW date |

### Major risk (1)

- **Pilot forever + claim drift:** free/cheap research eval never converts **or** counterpart pushes clinical-validation / DTC language.  
- **Mitigation:** written conversion clause day 0 of eval · weekly claim-language check · kill at the gate rather than extend.

### Kill criteria

1. Eval never converts to paid SOW / paid discovery by day 90.  
2. Clinical-claim / DTC drift (**RETRIEVED** F-RUO-5).  
3. Free pilot forever (no dated conversion clause).  
4. **Thesis kill:** zero eval interest after precision/speed proof ask → revisit rival (agility) — do not force Route B.

### Skeptical Q&A (paste-ready)

**Q: Thermo already sells ProcartaPlex hormone panels — why would they talk to Synvitta?**  
**A:** Thermo is a **peer incumbent**. Route B does not ask them to scrap ProcartaPlex or to **buy Synvitta**. We ask whether a **scoped research eval → paid custom / OEM SOW** fills a hormone-shaped gap they will not prioritize this quarter. If they will only engage as diligence/M&A with no eval→SOW path, that conversation is not our beachhead win.

**Q: Isn’t the Alachua buyout story just chase M&A?**  
**A:** No. Thermo’s experience buying companies here is **access / market familiarity**. The recommendation remains **eval→paid SOW**.

**Q: Why Abbott — do you have a UF Innovate partnership?**  
**A:** Public record shows Abbott licensed markers from **Banyan** (UF / Sid Martin graduate) — **RETRIEVED** ecosystem signal. A Synvitta↔Abbott warm path is **ESTIMATED** until Synvitta/UF confirms.

**Q: Isn’t “premium next-gen” just marketing?**  
**A:** Yes until the eval. Premium is the **price of a proof**, not a claimed H2H win. The eval sheet forces *their* specificity/TAT proxies; we will not invent peer-beating accuracy or cost numbers.

**Q: What if the eval never converts?**  
**A:** Kill the beachhead motion at day 90. Free pilot forever is an explicit kill — same as clinical-claim drift.

**Q: Are you recommending an FDA path?**  
**A:** No. We verify one assumption: research/dev + paid SOW under allowed claims. Filing strategy is out of scope.
