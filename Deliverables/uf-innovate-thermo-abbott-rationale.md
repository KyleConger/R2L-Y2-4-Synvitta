That is proof Thermo already knows how to engage UF-rooted companies in this geography — **access / familiarity rationale**, not “sell Synvitta.”

**Abbott (RETRIEVED ecosystem).** UF Innovate (*FDA Approves Blood Test Developed by Abbott, USAMMDA for Traumatic Brain Injury*, [innovate.research.ufl.edu/tba-blood-test](https://innovate.research.ufl.edu/tba-blood-test/)) reports FDA approval of an Army + **Abbott Point of Care** TBI blood test on Abbott’s **i-STAT Alinity**, using biomarkers from **UF startup Banyan Biomarkers**; Abbott received **licensure from Banyan in 2019** to put those markers on i-STAT. That is proof Abbott already partners with Sid Martin–class UF biotech — **ecosystem signal**, not a Synvitta↔Abbott deal on the public record.
