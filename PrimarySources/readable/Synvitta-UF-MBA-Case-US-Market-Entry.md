Synvitta Diagnostics  
U.S. Market Entry Challenge  |  UF Rise to Leadership MBA 
Company Background 
Synvitta Diagnostics is an early -stage biotechnology company based at UF Innovate | Sid Martin Biotech in Alachua, Florida. We 
develop laboratory test kits, compact testing instruments, and software. Our first commercial efforts have focused on Brazil;  we 
now need to decide how to build our business in the United States. 
Three Opportunities Under Consideration 
SynELISA™ | Research 
kits 
Three individual testing kits for Research Use Only (RUO): TSH and T4 (thyroid -related markers) and 
antibodies related to syphilis. Potential near -term sales to suitable research customers; not for 
patient diagnosis. 
SynArray™ | Newborn 
screening 
A multiplex platform in late -stage development intended to measure multiple newborn screening 
markers from one sample. Potential future clinical market, but additional development, validation 
and an appropriate regulatory pathway are required. 
SynArray™ | Custom 
panels 
The platform may also support tailored multi -marker tests for partners, such as SynHormone ™, a 
hormone-focused panel concept. Potential business models include research collaborations and 
paid co-development projects. 
The Real Business Challenge 
Synvitta has limited resources and does not currently have FDA authorization to market these products for clinical diagnostic  
use in the United States. Its challenge is to identify realistic near -term revenue and partnership opportunities while building 
toward future clinical commercialization. 
How should Synvitta enter the U.S. market with its current and developing products, without treating FDA authorization as 
something it already has? 
Important Regulatory Context — Plain English 
• RUO (Research Use Only): Products intended and marketed solely for genuine research activities, not for clinical diagnosis 
or treatment decisions. 
• CLIA: U.S. quality requirements for laboratories that test human specimens for clinical purposes. A CLIA-certified laboratory 
is not automatically authorized to market or use any research kit for patient diagnosis. 
• FDA: Oversees diagnostic devices and their intended uses. Clinical pathways, including qualifying laboratory -developed 
testing arrangements, require case -specific regulatory review. Students should identify questions to verify, not deliver legal 
opinions.

Your Role: MBA Consulting Team 
Recommend ONE priority opportunity for Synvitta to pursue first in the U.S. Consider the three product opportunities above, b ut 
do not create three full business plans. Assume approximately four hours for research, discussion and presentation preparation. 
Three Guiding Questions 
1. Where should Synvitta focus first? Choose SynELISA research kits, SynArray newborn -screening partnerships, or SynArray 
custom panel development. Compare business potential, readiness, barriers and speed to first revenue. 
2. What is a credible route to market? Identify an appropriate customer or partner type and a possible business model (RUO 
research sales, research evaluation or collaboration, custom development, or a laboratory partnership for expert regulatory 
assessment). Explain why it is appropriate and what cannot be assumed without FDA authorization. 
3. What should we do in the next 90 days? Propose three achievable actions, identify 2 –3 prospective U.S. customers or 
partners, and choose two simple metrics to track progress. 
Team Deliverable 
Prepare a 3 –5 slide presentation with: (1) recommended product opportunity and customer; (2) proposed commercial or 
partnership approach and the key regulatory assumption to verify; (3) a practical 90 -day plan with 2 –3 potential partners and 
TWO measures of progress. Highlight one major risk. No FDA filing strategy, detailed financial forecast or nationwide market -
sizing exercise is required. 
Questions to Explore (Not Assumptions) 
• Where can Synvitta legitimately offer RUO products today, and how could it reach research buyers? 
• Could collaborations with CLIA -certified laboratories or diagnostic developers help validate demand and develop future 
pathways? What regulatory and business facts must be checked first? 
• Would customized multiplex development provide a faster or more attractive initial business opportunity than selling 
standard kits?
